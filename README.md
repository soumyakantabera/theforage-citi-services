# Commercial Card Analytics Case Study: Client Needs Assessment to Executive Reporting

**Citi Treasury and Trade Solutions (TTS) Forage Virtual Experience Program**

---

## Project Overview

This is an educational case study I completed as part of the Citi Forage Virtual Experience Program (Treasury and Trade Solutions track). The program provided a series of realistic business scenarios, templates, and a synthetic transaction dataset. My task was to work through four connected exercises that simulate the kind of work a junior analyst or product analyst might do on Citi's commercial card team.

The project covers client discovery, product concept development, fee modeling, and executive-level reporting. All data and business context are simulated for learning purposes.

---

## What This Project Actually Is

- Educational simulation only. Not a real Citi product or production system.
- The transaction data (5,000 rows) was provided by the program. It is synthetic.
- The client ("Joe") and his business details were given as part of the case.
- Product features, pricing, and recommendations are based on the prompts and my analysis of the provided data.
- No real client data, no live systems, and no actual revenue impact.

---

## Tasks Completed

### Task 1: Client Needs Assessment
I drafted a short questionnaire for a long-standing client (a multi-location food franchise). The goal was to understand their current expense processes, pain points, and growth plans so a suitable commercial card product could be recommended later.

I used the email template and question-writing guidance provided in the program. The questionnaire mixes open questions with some structured options to keep it practical for the client to answer.

**Output:** Questionnaire email (DOCX and PDF)

---

### Task 2: Commercial Card Concept
Using the client insights from Task 1, I prepared a short slide deck introducing a proposed commercial card aimed at franchise businesses. The deck covers the main features, pricing structure, and a limited-time launch offer.

I kept the design clean and focused on the client's specific needs (expense control across many locations, future international expansion, and easier reconciliation).

**Output:** 4-slide pitch deck (PPTX)

---

### Task 3: Fee Modeling and Analysis
The program supplied a raw transaction file with 5,000 corporate card transactions. I built an Excel workbook that:

- Calculates fees using dynamic formulas (easy to change rates and see the impact immediately)
- Creates summary tables by client, region, payment type, and month
- Includes three charts (spend distribution by client, payment mode, and monthly trend)
- Compares Citi's current fee structure against two competitor cards

This was the most technical part. I focused on making the model transparent so someone could adjust the fee parameters and instantly see the revenue effect.

**Output:** Fee analysis workbook with charts (XLSX)

---

### Task 4: Leadership Insights Deck
I consolidated the findings from the previous tasks into a 5-slide presentation for senior leadership. The deck shows client concentration risk, payment mix opportunities, and a simple competitive fee comparison. It ends with three concrete recommendations based on the data.

I paid attention to chart placement, consistent formatting, and removing any unnecessary text so the key numbers and messages are easy to read in a meeting.

**Output:** 5-slide leadership presentation with embedded charts (PPTX)

---

## Limitations and Scope

This is a learning exercise with clear boundaries:

- All data is synthetic and was provided by the Forage program. It does not reflect real Citi clients or actual transaction volumes.
- The "FranchiseFlex Card" concept and all pricing/promotion ideas are illustrative only. They were created to complete the case requirements.
- No statistical modeling, forecasting, or advanced analytics were used beyond basic aggregation and charting.
- The recommendations are based solely on the provided dataset and case prompts. In a real role I would validate assumptions with actual stakeholders and additional data sources.
- The project demonstrates workflow and presentation skills rather than production system design.
